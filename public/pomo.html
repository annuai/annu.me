How it works

Simple by design.
Powerful by habit.

Pomo distills the Pomodoro Technique into three quiet steps — no friction, no fuss.

01

Add your tasks

Write down what matters today. Star the most important one — it'll always appear in your menu bar so you never lose the thread.

02

Start a session

25 minutes of pure focus. No notifications, no distractions. Pomo counts down quietly in the corner while you do the work.

03

Rest, then repeat

Take a short break. Then return. After four sessions, a longer rest. The rhythm builds momentum — and momentum builds great work.
